### Important evidence handling

The Chenab/IWT research page distinguishes:
1. treaty text and institutional records;
2. statements or submissions by Pakistan or India;
3. media-reported project proposals;
4. adjudicative awards/orders; and
5. user-supplied engineering figures.

Numerical design values supplied by a party or secondary source are displayed as reported positions unless the primary adjudicative/engineering record establishes a different operative value. The July 2027 Neutral Expert schedule supplied by the project owner is retained as a research note and marked for verification rather than presented as an independently verified binding schedule.
